Naukri Job Triage

A private assistant that reads job-alert emails from one Gmail inbox and drafts outreach to recruiters — every message is reviewed and approved by hand before anything is sent.

How it works
  1. 01

    Read Once a day, it searches the inbox for emails from naukri.com and pulls out the role, company, and recruiter contact where available.

  2. 02

    Draft A candidate outreach email is prepared — with a resume attached — and placed in a private review queue. Nothing leaves the inbox at this stage.

  3. 03

    Approve The owner reviews, edits, and only then sends each draft by hand from a private dashboard. The tool never sends on its own.
