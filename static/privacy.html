Privacy policy

Naukri Job Triage

Last updated August 2026

What this is

Naukri Job Triage is a personal tool built and used by a single individual, Prathamesh Patil, to help manage job-search outreach. It is not a public product, is not distributed to other users, and does not collect data from anyone other than its one owner.

What it accesses, and why

gmail.readonly Search and read emails sent from naukri.com, to find job alerts and extract role, company, and recruiter details. gmail.send Send an outreach email — but only at the exact moment the owner clicks "Approve & Send" on a specific, previously reviewed draft. Nothing is ever sent automatically.

What is stored

The tool keeps a private database of parsed job details (subject line, company, role, recruiter name/email, a link to the job posting) and a record of what was sent, skipped, or failed. It does not store full email bodies beyond what is needed to draft and thread a reply.

This data lives in a private database operated by the owner and is not accessible to anyone else.

What is never done

  • Data is not sold, shared, or shown to any third party.
  • No advertising or analytics tracking is attached to this data.
  • No email is ever sent without a manual, per-message approval from the owner.
  • Access is not used for anything beyond the owner's own job search.

Retention and deletion

Data is retained until the owner deletes it. Revoking this app's access from your Google Account settings stops all further access immediately.

Contact

Questions about this policy or this tool can be sent to pprathamesh2412@gmail.com.
